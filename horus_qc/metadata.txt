about=Horus QC is a QA/QC toolkit for teams that digitise and review buildings and roads from satellite imagery, grid by grid.

    It goes beyond general topology checkers:
    • 28 checks across buildings, roads and grids, measured in metres even on EPSG:4326 data
    • Overlaps are classified into duplicate / building inside another / real overlap / shared-wall sliver, so attached houses don't flood the list
    • Building-on-road, set-back, crossing-the-grid-edge and outside-all-grids checks
    • Road undershoots and overshoots (with auto-snap / auto-trim), dead ends, isolated pieces and T-junction-aware dangles
    • Near-square corner, spike and hairline-gap detection with one-click Orthogonalize / remove spike / snap wall
    • Review mode: a serpentine queue through your grid with keys to approve, flag and skip, a daily target counter, and review status saved in the data
    • Roof-to-base shift: pick the lean once per grid, then press 1-9 to shift a building by its floor count; WASD to nudge
    • Instant checks after every edit, background processing, an error marker layer, and CSV/GeoPackage export
    • All fixes are undoable (Ctrl+Z); nothing leaves your computer

    Overlap logic is ported from OVC (https://github.com/AmmarYasser455/ovc).
