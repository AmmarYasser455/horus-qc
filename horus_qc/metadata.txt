about=Horus QC helps teams that digitise and review buildings and roads from satellite imagery, grid by grid.

    Two simple steps:
    1 Check: one button finds problems in your grid (28 checks, measured in metres): duplicates, overlapping buildings, buildings on roads, buildings crossing the grid edge, broken outlines, roads that stop short of a junction, and more. Problems are grouped by type; click one to zoom to it and read what to do.
    2 Review: one button walks you through every building in your grid. Pick the roof-to-base lean once (V), then press 1-9 to move each building to its base by its number of floors, E to approve, F to flag. A daily target bar counts your work, and review status can be saved in the layer.

    Horus also warns you instantly when an edit creates a problem. Everything runs on your computer; all edits are undoable with Ctrl+Z.

    Overlap logic is ported from OVC (https://github.com/AmmarYasser455/ovc).
